420 SNOWY OWL AUXILIARY FIGHTER SQUADRON

No. 420 Squadron reformed at London, Ontario on 15 September 1948, and flew North American Mustang and Harvard aircraft in a fighter role until the squadron disbanded on 1 September 1956.

Honouring lives, stories, and sacrifice.
